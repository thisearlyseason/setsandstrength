# Landing page scroll effects

User selected lightweight layered effects and instructed us to continue. Preserve Sets & Strength branding, two-athlete hero, theme switch and honest coming-soon availability. Add neon lime smoke entering from beyond the viewport and crossing with scroll, perspective depth, benefit animations and clearer training benefits. Use CSS/SVG layers and small event-driven JavaScript; no WebGL or dependencies. Keep normal scrolling, readable copy, responsive layouts, no-JS content and reduced-motion support. Optimize hero delivery with responsive WebP assets. Existing checkout and local preview only; production publication is separate.

## Approaches
Lightweight layers selected for low loading cost; WebGL considered for realistic volumetric smoke but rejected for higher graphics demands. Motion uses a few transparent vector smoke layers, transformed on scroll, with perspective cards. Benefit copy describes existing planning, logging and progress features without promising results.
