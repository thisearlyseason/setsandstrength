removed annotation geometry, attachment patches, organs, nerves and ear
structures; converted centimeters to meters; spatially welded vertices;
recomputed normals; assigned materials, skin weights and an animation rig;
grouped surfaces by muscle region; exported glTF with split binary buffers.
